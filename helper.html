Bring iLearn into Course Manager

A small browser extension that reads your iLearn due dates, grades and announcements with your own login, and sorts each one into the right course for you.

The iLearn helper is installed in this browser.
Download the helper For Chrome or Edge on a computer · free · about 10 KB

What it does

  • Due dates, sortedEvery iLearn deadline lands in Tasks under its exact course, with no picking.
  • GradesNew grades show on Home and can be added to your grade calculator in one tap.
  • AnnouncementsEvery announcement from your courses, newest first, by course.
  • Course linksOne-tap links to each course’s iLearn page, for your section and for everyone.

Install it (about a minute)

  1. Download and unzip

    Use the button above, then unzip the file (Windows: right-click → Extract All; Mac: double-click). You’ll get a folder called ilearn-helper. Put it somewhere it can stay, like Documents: the browser loads the helper from that folder.

  2. Open your extensions page

    Type chrome://extensions into the address bar (Edge: edge://extensions) and press Enter.

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    The switch is at the top right in Chrome, and on the left in Edge. This only lets you add extensions from a folder.

  4. Click “Load unpacked”

    Choose the ilearn-helper folder (the one with manifest.json inside, not a folder around it). “Course Manager · iLearn helper” appears in the list. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu if you like.

  5. Log in to iLearn

    Open ilearn.aus.edu in the same browser and sign in.

  6. Open Course Manager

    Reload the site. Within a minute or two, Tasks → iLearn says Automatic sorting is on, and your calendar link is filled in for you.

Only courses on your timetable or with a syllabus in Course Manager are used. Dropped courses, older terms and advising spaces are left out.

Updating

  1. Download the new zip

    Unzip it over your old ilearn-helper folder, replacing the files.

  2. Reload it

    On chrome://extensions, press the ↻ button on the helper’s card.

Questions

Is it safe? What can it see?

It only works on ilearn.aus.edu and on Course Manager. It reads your courses, calendar, grades, feedback and announcements, using the login your browser already has. It never sees your password, never submits or changes anything on iLearn, and only sends what it reads to your own copy of Course Manager, plus any alerts you turn on.

Why isn’t it on the Chrome Web Store?

Installing from a folder is free and works the same. The only difference is that updates aren’t automatic: download the new zip and press reload.

How often does it check?

Due dates every couple of minutes, and grades and announcements every 30 minutes. It also checks within a few minutes whenever you’re on iLearn, so something you just submitted shows up quickly.

Can it alert me about new grades?

Yes. Turn on reminders in Course Manager (the bell next to your semester), and the helper sends an alert to your phone and computer when a grade or announcement is posted or a due date is added or moved. Pick which ones you want in the same place. It sends them while Chrome is running on your computer. The alert goes straight to your own devices through Course Manager’s reminder server, which only sees the alert itself.

It shows a red “!” on its icon

iLearn logged you out. Log in again at ilearn.aus.edu and it carries on, including anything you missed.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Browser extensions need Chrome or Edge on a computer. If you’re signed in to Course Manager, what the helper finds syncs to your phone too.

Chrome warns about developer-mode extensions

That’s normal for anything installed from a folder. You can keep it, or switch it off any time from chrome://extensions.
