      Add a semester

      Starts a fresh semester: courses, grades, timetable, tasks and finals start empty. Your current semester is kept, and you can switch back to it from the semester menu at the top.

      Upload

      Add any of these. They’re all optional, and each one fills in a different part of the site.

      1SyllabiPDF, Word, photos or text. Builds your grade calculators and adds exam and assignment dates to Tasks.
      2Class schedule (.ics)Exported from your student portal. Builds your timetable and the class countdown.

      Your program

      Catalog

      Your degree requirements follow this catalog. If you switched to a newer catalog, pick that one: the courses you’ve already taken stay where they are.

      When you started

      This only names your semesters, and it doesn’t have to match the catalog year. Importing a transcript sets it for you.

      Not listed, or want it read from your own catalog pages?
      1. Open your catalog PDF and find your major’s section.
      2. Save or print to PDF just the pages with your degree requirements and the proposed sequence of study (the year-by-year table). That’s usually 2–4 pages. Screenshots work too.
      3. Upload them here. The AI reads them and lays your program out as a flowchart. Check it afterwards: you can move, add or change any course.

      Connect iLearn

      Bring due dates from iLearn into Tasks. Only this semester’s courses are used: the iLearn course for everyone taking a class, your section’s course, and the same for labs.

      How to get your iLearn calendar link
      1. Sign in to iLearnGo to ilearn.aus.edu.
      2. Open CalendarFrom the main menu on the left, not from inside a course.
      3. Open Calendar settingsThe gear button at the top right of the calendar.
      4. Choose Share calendarIn the ⋯ menu next to the “Calendar settings” heading, then copy the link it shows. It usually ends in .ics.
      5. Paste it belowThe site checks it again whenever you open it, so new and moved due dates show up on their own.

      Older iLearn layout? Open Calendar and click Get External Calendar Link instead.

      Keep this link private. Anyone with it can see your iLearn deadlines, but not your grades or password.
