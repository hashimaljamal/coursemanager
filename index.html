      Final exams

      Upload your university’s final exam schedules: the common exams list and the regular schedule that maps class times to exam slots. You can pick both files at once. Courses whose syllabus has no final exam are left out.

      Add a semester

      Starts a fresh semester: courses, grades, timetable, tasks and finals start empty. Your current semester is kept, and you can switch back to it from the semester menu at the top.

      Upload

      Add any of these. They’re all optional, and each one fills in a different part of the site.

      1SyllabiPDF, Word, photos or text. Builds your grade calculators and adds exam and assignment dates to Tasks.
      2Class schedule (.ics)Exported from your student portal. Builds your timetable and the class countdown.
      3University academic calendarPDF, Word, photo or .ics. Sets your semester dates, breaks, holidays and make-up days.

      Which semester?

      This calendar covers more than one semester. Pick yours: its first and last day of classes fill in your semester, and its dates are added. Dates that have already passed come in marked as done.
